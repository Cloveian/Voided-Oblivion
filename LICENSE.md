## Additional permissions

These are grants on top of CC BY-NC-SA. They exist because a plain
NonCommercial licence would block things i actually want to happen.

### 1. The inter-tile interface is free for anyone, including commercially

The connector interface - the pinout, the 2.5mm pitch, the palindromic
ordering, the male/female gender rule, and the inter-tile protocol - is
dedicated to the public domain. Implement it in anything, sell whatever you
implement it in, no attribution required and no need to ask.

That means **submodules and accessories are unrestricted.** If you design your
own board that mates with a Voided Oblivion tile, that board is yours. Sell it
freely. The NonCommercial term applies to the tile design itself, not to things
that plug into it.

### 2. Small-scale selling is fine, no permission needed

**If you make less than US$1,000 in gross revenue per year from this design, the
NonCommercial term does not apply to you.** Sell boards, run a group buy at a
margin, build them for friends who pay you, whatever. You do not need to ask, you
do not need to tell me, and you do not owe me anything. Attribution and
ShareAlike still apply.

The point of the NonCommercial term is to stop a company mass-producing this for
profit. It is not there to stop a person selling a handful of boards.

### 3. Above that, just talk to me

Past US$1,000/year, open an issue on the repository and we will work something
out. I am not trying to prevent anyone from selling these - i would just like a
share if someone is making real money from my work. Expect a conversation about
a percentage, not a refusal.

The threshold is gross revenue from anything derived from this design, per
person or organisation, per calendar year. If you are unsure whether you are
over it, you are almost certainly not.
