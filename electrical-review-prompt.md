## Scope: electrical correctness, end to end
Use the `kicad` skill's full design-review contract (all applicable analyzers via `--analysis-dir analysis/`, cross-analysis, EMC, thermal, SPICE — ngspice is installed), fresh `kicad-cli pcb drc --severity-all` and `kicad-cli sch erc`, plus schematic↔PCB parity. Prior analyzer runs exist in `analysis/` (2026-08-08 and 2026-08-16) — diff against them. Then go beyond the analyzers:

**Per-IC datasheet verification (the core of this review).** ~50 datasheets are in `Refrences/datasheets/` (note the spelling; some historical mislabels are documented in the log). For EVERY active part: verify symbol pin map against the datasheet pin table (custom symbols in `Voided-Oblivion-misc` are the highest risk — LM74700 DDF and NCE4009S were already verified 2026-08-16, the rest were not re-verified after the respin), abs-max and recommended operating ranges on every pin vs the worst-case rail voltage it can actually see (PD+ can be 20 V; BS+ worst case 5.95 V; VBUS can be dragged to ~19.3 V through Q2's body diode when a neighbor powers PD+ — check everything hanging on VBUS against that), required external components and values vs the recommended application circuit, and enable/UVLO/PG behavior.

**Walk the bring-up order explicitly.** This project found four cold-start latches, all "a rail depending on something downstream of itself" — steady-state review misses this class. Walk: cold attach at vSafe5V → comparator trip → PD negotiation to 9 V/20 V → handoff (Q1 opens, Q2 closes, +5VA EN) → LDO/3V3 → bucks → gated +5VP → submodule chain (BS+→U12 / +5VP→U15 → SM BUS → U16 → SM+). Also walk the reverse: hot-unplug, neighbor-tile hotplug into an enabled edge, PD renegotiation mid-operation, one port unplugged while the second is attached, and the dual-port arbitration (TS3USB30E + 2× FUSB302, CC1/CC2 crossed on both ports — deliberate).

**Worst-case and tolerance analysis.** Comparator trip window (R30/R31/R22/R46 + TLV431B) vs vSafe5V 5.5 V floor and LM66100 6.0 V abs max — redo the RSS budget with the fitted tolerances and actual MPNs. Check every MPN field against its Value field (a stale-MPN bug was already caught on R30 — 44.2k MPN on a 35.7k value; verify it got fixed and hunt for the same bug elsewhere). Cap voltage derating on PD+/VBUS (20 V rail: X5R/X7R DC bias at the fitted case sizes). Current: per-edge 4 A ceiling vs FET/track/via/pour capacity on the as-routed board; TPS54302 inductor Isat vs current limit; LDO dissipation at real load.

**The ADC signal chain** (the product IS this chain): GH39F → mux → AM0/AM1 → RP2350 ADC. Source impedance vs sampling time, mux Ron/injection, settling at the scan rate, the ratiometric assumption, and whether the F6 ADC_AVDD filter provision ever landed (it was the last unquantified item — check pad 59 on the routed board).

**RP2350 specifics:** E9 erratum — every pin whose default-low state is load-bearing needs an external pull-down ≤8.2 k (project rule: 4.7 k); audit all of them (inter-tile Rx detects, all load-switch ENs, boot straps). QSPI flash wiring, USB_BOOT strap, crystal load caps, VREG components, IOVDD/DVDD/ADC_AVDD decoupling per the hardware design guide (both PDFs are in Refrences/).

**Interacting-pair checks:** shared rails (combined load vs source at each PD tier, the 80% rule), both I²C buses (pull-up values/ownership — FUSB302 INT pull-ups were flagged at 100k vs 4.7k typ once, verify what's fitted), the SN74LVC2T45 level shifter (Ioff/partial-power-down direction, A-port-driven-at-all-times obligation), and the `!firmware-note!` hardware/firmware contract items in the docs — confirm the hardware side of each still holds on the final board.
