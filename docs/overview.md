# Voided Oblivion

## What is this project?
This is a keyboard I have started, stopped, and restarted 3 times, but this time is different. This time I'ma actually write out my thoughts and design processes, so I can demonstrate my skills and abilities to future employers / college applications.
Also writing out my decisions will also make me more likely to not try and over achieve/cram as much stuff on this that can possibly fit.

The last 3 attempts didn't die because they didn't work, they stalled because i got too busy to finish, and by the time i came back i'd lost the thread and basically restarted, usually because i'd tunneled on a detail or wanted to cram in a new feature. So the real enemy here isn't technical, it's time. i'd get too busy, drop it, and by the time i came back i'd lost the thread. i've got the time this round, so writing everything down covers the rest: decisions stay made, and i can pick back up instead of starting over.

## What I'm working with
- Voron 2.4 (350mm$^{3}$) FDM printer
- Anycubic Photon mono x2 resin 3d printer
- Multiple soldering irons + hot air + 200mm$^{2}$ PCB reflow hotplate (SMD assembly)
- CNC that can in theory cut aluminum (never tested)
- Access to a very accurate laser powerful enough to cut aluminum

**Budget:** i'd like to stay around ~$250, but that's a guideline to keep me honest, not a hard limit.

## Background
This isn't my first keyboard. i've designed a Corne-shaped board in KiCad before, running a nice!nano v2 with Choc switches, per-key RGB, and resin-printed keycaps (back when i only had a worse-than-an-Ender-3 FDM printer and my resin one). So KiCad, per-key RGB, and printing my own caps are all known territory.

i'm also really good at 3D design in Fusion 360 (like, commission-level), and i daily-drive Arch.
